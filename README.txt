GRAINHUB GRAIN MARKET WEBSITE

Files:
- index.html: website structure and content
- style.css: responsive design and styling
- script.js: mobile menu, current year, and demo contact form

How to run:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open the folder.
3. Double-click index.html to open it in a browser.

Notes:
- The grain rates shown are sample placeholder values only, not live market rates.
- Replace the sample email and phone number with your business contact details.
- Contact form is front-end demo only; it does not send messages to a server.
- Some photos and fonts load from external internet services.
